AUTOMOTIVE SERVICE REPAIR MANUAL - MANUAL ID: MNL-24-001
VEHICLE MODEL: 2023 SUV Model X
SYSTEM: IGNITION SYSTEM & CYLINDER MISFIRE DIAGNOSTICS (DTC P0300)

1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
The ignition system utilizes individual coil-on-plug assemblies for cylinders 1 through 6.
Diagnostic Trouble Code P0300 indicates a random or multiple cylinder misfire event detected by the Engine Control Unit (ECU).

2. DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURES
- Step 1: Connect OBD-II scanner and record freeze frame data.
- Step 2: Disconnect negative battery cable and wait 5 minutes before servicing electrical components.
- Step 3: Remove decorative engine cover (10mm bolts).
- Step 4: Inspect Bank 1 ignition coil assemblies (cylinders 1, 3, 5 located on passenger side).
- Step 5: Test primary resistance across terminals 1 and 2. Specification: 0.4 to 0.6 Ohms.
- Step 6: Test secondary resistance across output tower and terminal 2. Specification: 5.0 to 7.0 kOhms.
