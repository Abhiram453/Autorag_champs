# TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN (TSB-22-112)

**Subject:** Wiring Harness Connector C102 Corrosion Inspection  
**Bulletin ID:** TSB-22-112  
**Date:** October 24, 2023  
**Applicable Models:** Series X / Global, 2023 SUV Model X  

---

### 1. Condition
Customers may experience intermittent random misfire illumination (DTC P0300 or P0301) accompanied by hesitation under acceleration.

### 2. Cause
Moisture intrusion at the engine compartment bulkhead wiring harness connector C102 leading to pin resistance variance.

### 3. Service Action
1. Inspect connector C102 located on the right bulkhead (passenger side).
2. Look for green verdigris or corrosion on pins 4, 5, and 12.
3. If corrosion is found, replace connector pigtail harness kit part # PRT-882-BRK.
4. Apply dielectric grease to connector seals prior to mating.
