MANDATORY SAFETY RECALL NOTICE

Recall ID: RCL-23-088B

Component: Battery Control Module Firmware Update

Affected Vehicles: Series Y / NA & 2023 SUV Model X (AWD Hybrid)

Summary of Safety Defect

On certain 2023 SUV Model X vehicles, software in the High Voltage Battery Control Module (BCM) may incorrectly calculate cell thermal limits during rapid charging. This may cause false battery disconnect errors or thermal management warnings.

Remedy & Service Procedure

  • Flash Battery Control Module software to version v1.0.0 or higher.
  • Perform high voltage isolation test prior to vehicle release.
  • Ensure diagnostic session ID is logged in the compliance audit panel.
